Services — Sales

Sales Expert

Pipeline, process, and playbooks — from a guy who’s carried the number, run the region, and built the BD engine.

I’ve been VP of Business Development at SEIDOR. I ran the Northeast region as General Manager at Vision33. Today I’m Head of Sales for Quaint Business Solutions. I’m also a member of the Forbes Business Development Council. I say all that not to impress you — but so you know the advice comes from the chair you’re sitting in, not from a textbook.

Most SMB sales problems aren’t talent problems. They’re system problems. No defined pipeline stages. No follow-up cadence. No qualification criteria, so the forecast is fiction. Prospecting that happens when someone feels like it. I fix the system, and the system fixes the numbers.

What I build

Pipeline architecture. Stages that mean something — defined by buyer actions, not seller hope. Entry and exit criteria for every stage, so a deal in “proposal” actually means something and your forecast stops being astrology.

BDR playbooks. Prospecting is a process, not a personality trait. Ideal customer profile, list-building, outreach sequences across email/phone/social, objection handling, and the handoff to closers — documented so it works when your best rep is on vacation and when you hire rep number three.

Qualification discipline. The most expensive activity in sales is working deals that were never going to close. We install qualification early and make “no” a win — because a fast no is worth ten slow maybes.

Sales management rhythm. Weekly pipeline reviews that aren’t interrogations. Metrics that predict instead of just report: activity, conversion by stage, cycle length, average deal size. You’ll know what’s broken before the quarter ends, not after.

What’s included

  • Sales audit: pipeline, process, tools, and team — what’s working, what’s theater
  • Pipeline redesign with stage definitions and exit criteria
  • BDR prospecting playbook: ICP, sequences, scripts, and handoff rules
  • Qualification framework your team will actually use
  • CRM setup or cleanup so the data reflects reality
  • Management cadence: pipeline reviews, coaching, and forecasting that holds up

Who it’s for

SMB owners and sales leaders whose revenue depends on a process nobody wrote down. You have reps — maybe great ones — but results swing wildly month to month, the forecast is a guess, and growth means hiring more people instead of making the machine better. If your best salesperson is also your only process, we should talk.

FAQ

Do you do fractional sales leadership, or one-time projects?

Both. Some companies need the full build — audit, pipeline, playbooks, then handoff. Others need an ongoing hand on the wheel: weekly pipeline reviews, rep coaching, forecast calls. We’ll figure out which one fits on the first call.

Will this work with my CRM?

The process comes first; the tool serves the process. I’ll work with what you have — and if what you have is an inbox and a prayer, we’ll fix that too. Bad CRM data is a symptom; the disease is no process. We cure the disease.

Can you train my existing team, or do I need to hire?

Usually the team you have is fine — they’ve just never been given a real system. Training and coaching are part of the engagement. If someone genuinely isn’t cut out for the role, I’ll tell you that straight too. No sunk-cost sentimentality.

How is this different from a sales training course?

Training teaches skills to individuals. I build the system the individuals operate in — pipeline, playbooks, metrics, management rhythm. Skills fade; systems compound. Ideally you get both, but the system is what moves the number.

Your pipeline should be a machine, not a mood.
