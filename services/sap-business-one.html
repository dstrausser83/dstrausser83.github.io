FAQ

Is SAP Business One overkill for a small business?

Sometimes — and I’ll tell you when. B1 is a serious system with serious implementation behind it. If you’re a 10-person services firm, Odoo is probably the better fit. If you’re a distributor with three warehouses or a manufacturer with real production complexity, B1 is exactly the right weight class. Fit first, brand second.

What does a B1 implementation really cost?

Licenses plus implementation services plus data migration plus training plus add-ons — and the internal time your team invests. I won’t quote you a number on a webpage because any number I put here would be fiction for your specific situation. What I will do: build you honest TCO math on a call, all five buckets, no lowball. Read how TCO math works first.

Our current B1 partner stopped being responsive. Can you help?

Yes — this is more common than you’d think, and it’s one of the reasons I do what I do. We assess where your system stands, stabilize what’s critical, and either get the project back on track or plan the path forward. You don’t have to stay married to a partner that ghosted you.

How long does implementation take?

A well-scoped SMB implementation runs in months. The variables are scope clarity, data cleanliness, and how available your team is for decisions and testing. I’d rather give you a realistic timeline on a call than a fantasy number here — see my post on implementation timeline math for why the industry’s numbers can’t be trusted.
