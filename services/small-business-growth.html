Services — Growth

Small Business Growth

Growth isn’t a tactic. It’s systems, sales, and marketing working together — instead of stepping on each other.

Most small businesses don’t have a growth problem. They have a everything-fighting-everything problem. Sales is winging it without a process. Marketing is random acts of posting. The systems underneath — the spreadsheets, the inbox-as-CRM, the “ask Linda, she knows” institutional knowledge — can’t support the growth you’re trying to create. So every push for more revenue just creates more chaos.

I help SMB owners get off the hamster wheel. We find the bottlenecks — the places where leads die, where fulfillment chokes, where the numbers lie to you — and we fix them in the right order. Systems first, so growth doesn’t break the business. Then sales and marketing, aimed at the same target, running on the same data.

Here’s the thing most advisors won’t tell you: growth doesn’t fix a broken business, it exposes it. More leads into a leaky pipeline just means more leaks. More orders into shaky fulfillment means more angry customers. That’s why we sequence the work — stop the bleeding, fix the foundation, then pour fuel on it. Anybody who tells you to scale first and fix later is selling you the chaos you already have, just bigger.

Does this sound familiar? The usual suspects

After 10+ years working with SMBs, the growth killers are almost always the same:

Spreadsheet operations. The business runs on Excel, email threads, and one person’s memory. Nothing scales, nothing’s visible, and every report is an archaeology project. The fix is a real system of record — often an ERP like Odoo or SAP Business One — but the fix starts with admitting the spreadsheets have to go.

Sales without a process. Deals live in someone’s head or inbox. Follow-up is vibes-based. Nobody can tell you the close rate, the average cycle length, or which lead source actually pays. See Sales Expert — pipeline and process are fixable, and they’re usually the fastest win.

Marketing without a story. Posting when someone remembers, boosting the occasional ad, wondering why nothing compounds. Marketing needs the brand foundation from Dead Brand Stuff and a content engine that ships — see Marketing Expert.

No numbers you trust. You can’t grow what you can’t measure. If the financials, the pipeline, and the operational data don’t agree with each other, every decision is a guess. Systems fix this. Everything else is decoration.

The owner is the bottleneck. Every decision waits for you. Every key customer relationship lives in your head. Every process has an unwritten step that only you know. It worked when you were small; now it’s the ceiling. The fix isn’t working harder — it’s building systems and people that work without you, so the business can grow past the size of your calendar.

Growth broke fulfillment. You sold it — congratulations — and now delivery is late, quality is slipping, and the customers you fought to win are wondering if they made a mistake. Selling ahead of operations is how good companies get bad reputations. We fix the operations side before we pour on the sales and marketing, so growth builds the business instead of breaking it.

Who it’s for

US small and midsize businesses — typically the owner-led companies doing real revenue but running on duct tape. You’re past startup chaos but not yet at “we have a COO and a dashboard” maturity. You know something’s holding you back; you just can’t see it from inside the building.

Not for: companies looking for a magic tactic. If you want someone to promise 10x from a funnel hack, I’m the wrong guy and I’ll tell you so on the first call.

FAQ

Do I need new software, or can we fix this with what we have?

Sometimes what you have is fine and it’s the process that’s broken. Sometimes you’re asking a spreadsheet to do an ERP’s job and no amount of process will fix it. The audit tells us which it is before anyone spends a dollar on software. I don’t sell software on this engagement — I tell you the truth about what you need.

How fast will we see results?

The quick wins — follow-up discipline, killing an obvious bottleneck, cleaning up the pipeline — usually show up in weeks. The systems work takes months. Anyone promising overnight transformation is selling you something. Growth compounds; it doesn’t teleport.

Will this disrupt my team?

Less than the chaos is already disrupting them. We sequence the work so the business keeps running — quick wins first to build trust, bigger changes with the team involved, not ambushed. I’ve run implementations; I know what change fatigue looks like and how to avoid it.

Can you also implement the systems you recommend?

Yes. Through Quaint Business Solutions I implement Odoo and SAP Business One, and I do automation and AI work for the gaps in between. One throat to choke, one brain holding the whole picture.
