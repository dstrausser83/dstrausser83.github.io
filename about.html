About Us

We’re Dead Brands.

A family-oriented small business with a loud personality that gets shit done.

Who we are

Dead Brands, LLC is a small crew with a big engine, based in Pottsville, Pennsylvania. We’re family-oriented to the core — this business runs on the same values as the dinner table: show up, tell the truth, and take care of your people.

We’re loud on purpose. In a world of beige agencies and 40-slide decks, we’d rather say the thing nobody else will say and then do the work nobody else will do. Our team is a mix of human and virtual — real people setting the vision, backed by an AI-powered crew that researches, builds, and follows through at machine speed, around the clock.

What we do: revive the brands of yesterday and build the businesses of tomorrow. Too many good companies are running on a great business and a tired brand — the story they tell the world stopped being true somewhere around 2014. We fix the positioning, the messaging, and the content engine that keeps the story alive. And for the inside of the business, we bring serious ERP firepower — Odoo and SAP Business One — to small and midsize companies that can’t afford to get their systems wrong.

What we stand for

Family first

We’re a family-oriented business. The people we work with get treated like people we’ll see at the cookout — because around here, that’s not far from the truth.

The founder’s road here

Most ERP consultants started in IT. David started in another country. He spent 15 years living in Mexico — long enough to become fluent in Spanish and long enough to serve as a binational advisor to Baja California’s Secretary of Tourism, helping American companies bring products into Latin America. That work taught him something most tech people never learn: business is done across cultures, currencies, and time zones, and the people who win are the ones who actually listen before they pitch.

He’s spent 10+ years inside the ERP world from every angle that matters — including as General Manager of the Northeast region at Vision33, a leading global SAP Business One partner, and as VP of Business Development at SEIDOR. He’s been the guy in the vendor’s chair, so he knows every trick in the book. Now he works for you. Today he’s contracted as Head of Sales for Quaint Business Solutions, a boutique ERP consulting firm implementing SAP Business One and Odoo for US small and midsize businesses.

Off the clock he’s a single dad — the organizing principle of his life — a guitarist still chasing the rock star dream, and a Philadelphia Eagles fan through every season.
