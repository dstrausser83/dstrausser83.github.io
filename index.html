About

The short version

I’m David — CEO of Dead Brands, LLC, my own company. Through Dead Brands I’m contracted as Head of Sales for Quaint Business Solutions, bringing SAP Business One & Odoo and smart business technology to small and midsize companies. I’m a business development pro and ERP veteran of over a decade — before this, I ran the Northeast region as General Manager at Vision33 — the number-one global partner for SAP Business One — and served as VP of Business Development at SEIDOR.

My road here wasn’t the usual one. I’m a Penn State grad who spent 15 years living in Mexico (fluent Spanish, by the way), where I served as a binational advisor to Baja California’s Secretary of Tourism, helping American companies bring products into Latin America. That’s where I learned that business is business in any language — and that the best technology is the kind people actually use.

I also host the Shark Bite Biz podcast — 200+ episodes on the 3 G’s: personal growth, professional growth, and business growth. Because in a world full of sharks, you learn how to bite first. I’m a member of the Forbes Business Development Council and the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council.

Through changing times — from digital transformation to now AI — I’ve stayed with the ever-evolving landscape to enable small businesses to start winning, and start winning now. ERP projects have a reputation for going sideways. Mine don’t — because I’ve seen every way they can break, and I plan for all of them. If your systems are held together with spreadsheets and hope, we should talk.

Off the clock, I’m a single dad, a guitarist still chasing the rock star dream, and a Philadelphia Eagles fan through the bad years and the parades. Life’s a blessing, work should be fun, and the volume knob goes to eleven.

Dead Brands, LLC is my company — the home base for the podcast, the content, and the mission: reviving the brands of yesterday, building the businesses of tomorrow.
