About

The short version

I’m David — CEO of Dead Brands, LLC, my own company. Through Dead Brands I’m contracted as head of sales for Quaint Business Solutions, bringing SAP Business One and smart business technology to small and midsize companies. Before that, I ran the Northeast region as General Manager at Vision33 — the number-one global partner for SAP Business One.

My road here wasn’t the usual one. I’m a Penn State grad who spent 15 years living in Mexico (fluent Spanish, by the way), where I served as a binational advisor to Baja California’s Secretary of Tourism, helping American companies bring products into Latin America. That’s where I learned that business is business in any language — and that the best technology is the kind people actually use.

I also host the Shark Bite Biz podcast — 200+ episodes on the 3 G’s: personal growth, professional growth, and business growth. Because in a world full of sharks, you learn how to bite first. I’m a member of the Forbes Business Development Council and the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council, and I run my own media company, Dead Brands LLC.

ERP projects have a reputation for going sideways. Mine don’t — because I’ve seen every way they can break, and I plan for all of them. If your systems are held together with spreadsheets and hope, we should talk.

Off the clock, I’m a single dad, a guitarist still chasing the rock star dream, and a Philadelphia Eagles fan through the bad years and the parades. Life’s a blessing, work should be fun, and the volume knob goes to eleven.
