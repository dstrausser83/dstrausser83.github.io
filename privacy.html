Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it — in plain English.

Who we are

Dead Brands, LLC — 213 N. Centre St. #1, Pottsville, PA 17901. Contact: david@deadbrands.co.

What we collect

  • Mailing list: the name and email address you type into our signup form. Nothing is added without you submitting it.
  • Orders: when you buy merch, we (and our print-on-demand partner Printify) receive your name, email, shipping address, and order details — only what's needed to print and ship your order. Payment details go to the payment processor, not to us.
  • Messages you send us: whatever you include when you email us or use a contact form.
  • Analytics — only if you click Accept: Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps and anonymized session recordings), our own anonymous pageview beacon, and the B2B visitor-identification tools RB2B and Leadfeeder. None of this loads until you accept the cookie banner. Full details are in our Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • To print, ship, and support your merch orders.
  • To send the newsletter and updates you signed up for (unsubscribe anytime — every email has a link).
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To understand which pages and products get attention so we can improve the site.
  • To follow up with businesses that visit our B2B pages.

Who we share it with

  • Printify — our print-on-demand partner, which fulfills merch orders (printing and shipping). They receive the order details needed to do that, under their own privacy terms.
  • Analytics providers — Google, Microsoft, RB2B, and Leadfeeder, only after you accept cookies, under their terms.

We never sell your personal information. Ever. No data brokers, no ad networks, no cross-site trackers.

Your rights

Ask us what we have on you, correct it, or delete it: email david@deadbrands.co and we'll handle it. Unsubscribe from the mailing list anytime via the link in any email.

How long we keep it

Mailing list data stays until you unsubscribe. Order records are kept as long as tax and business records require. Analytics data follows each provider's retention settings.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date below. The current version always lives at this page.

Questions

Contact: david@deadbrands.co — Dead Brands, LLC, 213 N. Centre St. #1, Pottsville, PA 17901.
