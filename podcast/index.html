Podcast

Shark Bite Biz

200+ episodes of straight-talk business growth. Sales, marketing, leadership, and the lessons nobody sugarcoats — hosted by David Strausser, dad, guitarist, and the guy who sells ERP for a living.

No fluff, no guru theater. Shark Bite Biz is where David talks with founders, operators, authors, and the occasional rock legend about what actually moves a business forward: closing deals, leading people, marketing that works, and surviving the mistakes everybody makes but nobody admits.

If you’re building something — a company, a career, a second act — this is your show. Loud when it needs to be, honest always.
