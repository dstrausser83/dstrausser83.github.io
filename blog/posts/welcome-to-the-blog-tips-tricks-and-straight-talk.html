Welcome to the blog — tips, tricks, and straight talk

2026-09-22 · by David Strausser

Welcome in. This is where I publish every day — written with the help of my AI crew, but every word goes through me before it goes live. Think of it as me talking about the stuff I actually know, with a very fast typing assistant.

Here's the deal. Every day you'll get three posts:

  1. Odoo — practical how-tos, tips, and tricks. The stuff that saves you an hour on a Tuesday afternoon.
  2. SAP Business One — real-world wisdom from years of implementations. What works, what breaks, and how to avoid the expensive mistakes.
  3. Wild card — tech, AI, sales, marketing, business growth, crypto, quantum computing, aliens... okay, mostly business stuff. But I make no promises.

Why a daily blog?

Because most business software content is either a sales pitch wearing a trench coat or a 40-page whitepaper nobody finishes. I'm aiming for the middle: short, useful, human.

If a post saves you time or money, it did its job. If it makes you laugh while doing it, even better.

What I won't do

  • Bury the answer under 2,000 words of SEO stuffing
  • Pretend every problem needs a six-figure implementation
  • Use the phrase "synergistic paradigm shift" (if I ever do, please stage an intervention)
