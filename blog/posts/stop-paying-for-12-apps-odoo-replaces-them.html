You're Paying for 12 Apps. Odoo Replaces All of Them.

2026-09-22 · by David Strausser

Quick answer

The average SMB pays for 10–15 disconnected SaaS apps that don't talk to each other. Odoo replaces most of them — CRM, accounting, inventory, e-commerce, marketing, helpdesk, project management — in one integrated system, usually for less than you're paying for three of your current subscriptions.

Do a quick audit. Count your subscriptions: CRM, email marketing, accounting, inventory, project management, helpdesk, e-commerce platform, HR, document signing... I'll wait. Most small businesses I talk to are paying for 10 to 15 apps, and here's the dirty secret: half of them overlap, none of them integrate properly, and your team re-enters the same data in three places.

The hidden cost isn't the subscriptions

The subscription fees are just the visible part. The real cost is:

  • Double data entry. Customer info in the CRM, again in accounting, again in the shipping tool. Every copy is a chance for errors.
  • Integration spaghetti. Zapier zaps holding your business together with digital duct tape. When one breaks, nobody notices for a week.
  • No single source of truth. Sales says one number, accounting says another, and the truth is in a spreadsheet on someone's desktop.
  • Training overhead. Every app has its own login, its own UI, its own quirks. New hires need a week just to learn where things live.

What Odoo actually replaces

One system, one database, one login:

  • CRM & Sales — pipeline, quotes, orders (replaces your CRM + quoting tool)
  • Accounting & Invoicing — real double-entry accounting (replaces QuickBooks/Xero for many)
  • Inventory & Purchase — warehouses, barcodes, reordering rules
  • E-commerce & Website — built-in storefront and site builder
  • Email & Marketing Automation — campaigns, segmentation, drip flows
  • Project & Timesheets — task tracking, billing by the hour
  • Helpdesk & Knowledge Base — support tickets that link to the actual customer record
  • HR, Time Off, Recruitment — the back-office basics

When your helpdesk ticket links directly to the customer's order history, invoice status, and lifetime value — without an integration — that's when you feel the difference.

The math

Add up your current SaaS spend. For most 10–50 person companies, it's $1,000–$3,000/month across all those apps. Odoo Enterprise for the same headcount typically runs a fraction of that — and every app shares one database, so the integration cost drops to zero.

Frequently asked questions

Is Odoo really free?

Odoo Community is free and open source — genuinely useful for CRM, sales, and basic needs. Odoo Enterprise (the full app suite with upgrades and mobile) is paid per user per month, and still dramatically cheaper than a stack of point solutions. Try it free for 15 days: quaintbusiness.com/tryodoo.

What if I love one of my current apps?

Keep it. Odoo doesn't demand purity — it has an API and connectors for the tools worth keeping. The goal is killing the redundant 80%, not ripping out the one tool your team genuinely loves.

How hard is it to migrate from multiple apps?

It's a project, not a weekend — but it's one migration instead of maintaining twelve integrations forever. Data imports are straightforward, and you do it once. I've guided companies through it; the pattern is well understood.

Does consolidating create vendor lock-in?

Less than you think. Your data exports cleanly, and Odoo's open-source roots mean you're never truly trapped. Compare that to your current situation: locked into a dozen vendors simultaneously.
