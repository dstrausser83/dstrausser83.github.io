Purish: From Paper and Excel to Same-Day Shipping with Odoo

Berlin cosmetics distributor Purish replaced paper-and-Excel warehouse operations with Odoo Inventory, Purchase and Accounting — doubling purchase-to-invoice efficiency and shipping near same-day even during peak sale volumes.

The challenge: paper can’t scale

Purish distributes 7 international cosmetics brands into German drugstore chains, runs its own webshop, and sells on marketplaces like Amazon and Douglas — all coordinated on paper and Excel in the warehouse. Peak season meant chaos: slow fulfillment, weeks of training for temp staff, and no real-time picture of stock anywhere.

For a distributor, the warehouse is the business. When it runs on paper, every growth spurt becomes a stress test you fail in public.

The solution: Odoo across the warehouse

With Odoo partner bobco GmbH, Purish implemented Odoo Inventory, Purchase and Accounting, digitizing the warehouse end to end — from purchase order to invoice, with real stock visibility across every sales channel: drugstore chains, webshop, and marketplaces alike.

The results: speed as a feature

Purchase-to-invoice processes run 100% more efficiently. Orders ship near same-day even during peak Summer Sale volumes. And seasonal temp staff — once a weeks-long training burden — are productive in hours.

That last number is the sleeper: when onboarding takes hours instead of weeks, peak season stops being a staffing crisis and starts being a revenue opportunity.

The SMB takeaway

Distributors live and die on fulfillment speed. Purish proves you don’t need an enterprise WMS to get enterprise-grade throughput — you need one system where inventory, purchasing and accounting actually talk to each other.
