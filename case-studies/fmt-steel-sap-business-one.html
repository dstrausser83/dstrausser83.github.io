FMT Steel: 300% Growth on SAP Business One

A steel products manufacturer replaced error-prone manual processes with SAP Business One — including a full quality management system — and grew the business 300% while becoming 500% more profitable.

The challenge: growth without structure

FMT Steel was growing, but its processes weren’t. Manual workflows and spreadsheet-driven operations meant errors crept into orders, production, and finance. And as export volumes climbed, getting export tax calculations right became a constant fire drill — the kind of problem that doesn’t just cost money, it caps how fast you’re allowed to grow.

This is the classic mid-market manufacturing trap: the business outgrows its systems, but everyone’s too busy fighting fires to fix the systems. Sound familiar?

The solution: SAP Business One, end to end

The company implemented SAP Business One, including a dedicated quality management system, to bring discipline to every process from order to delivery. Tribal knowledge and manual checks were replaced with defined, system-enforced workflows — and accurate export tax calculations became automatic instead of aspirational.

For a manufacturer, the quality management piece matters as much as the financials: when the system enforces the process, defects and rework drop, and customers feel it.

The results: the numbers

300% business growth. 500% more profitable since implementation. Errors dropped because the system catches them before they ship; exports flow because tax handling is built into the workflow instead of bolted on at month-end.

The SMB takeaway

Manufacturers in the $5M–$250M range often assume ERP is for the big guys. FMT Steel proves the opposite: process discipline is what creates the big guys. If your shop still runs on spreadsheets and heroics, this is what the other side of that decision looks like.
